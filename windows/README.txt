kdtv for Windows
================

Live TV, movies, series and YouTube from your IPTV provider: watch, record,
schedule recordings and download.

START
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Documents is fine).
  2. Double-click kdtv.exe.
  3. The first time, type the login your provider gave you: the server
     address, your username and your password. kdtv checks it and downloads
     your channels, movies and TV guide (about a minute).

KEYS
  Arrows to move, Enter to open or watch, Esc to go back.
  1 to 9 jump to a section, / searches, ? shows every key, q quits.
  On a channel: Enter watch, r record, n record the next show.
  On a movie or episode: Enter watch, d download.

WHERE THINGS GO
  Recordings:  your Videos folder, in kdtv
  Downloads:   your Videos folder, in kdtv\Downloads
  Your login and lists stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\kdtv. Unzipping a newer kdtv
  somewhere else keeps them.

SCHEDULED RECORDINGS
  kdtv starts them itself, so keep kdtv open and the computer on. kdtv
  stops the computer from going to sleep while a recording is coming. If
  kdtv is closed at that time, the recording is skipped.

ONE CONNECTION
  Most providers allow one connection at a time. Watching, recording and
  downloading share it: downloads pause by themselves while you record or
  watch, then pick up where they stopped.

CHANGE YOUR LOGIN
  Account (8), then e.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Status (9) shows what kdtv did today. Take a screenshot and send it.
  The details are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\kdtv\data\logs.

Included programs: FFmpeg (ffmpeg.org), mpv (mpv.io) and yt-dlp
(github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp), free software under their own licenses.
